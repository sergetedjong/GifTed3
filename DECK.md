# Gifted XAF Wallet — Investor Deck (12 slides)

**1. Title & Vision**  
A compliance-first Web3 wallet for XAF markets, enabling fast, low-cost USDC payments with Mobile Money cash-out.

**2. Problem (XAF Remittances & Friction)**  
- High cross-border fees; settlement delays.  
- Fragmented rails; limited access for freelancers/SMBs.  
- Cash dependency despite high mobile-money penetration.

**3. Regulatory Reality (CEMAC/COBAC)**  
- Banks/MFIs restricted from crypto facilitation.  
- Our design: non-custodial wallet + licensed PSPs for fiat legs.  
- Clear AML/KYC posture; sanctions/PEP screening.

**4. Solution (Live Demo on Vercel)**  
- Create/connect wallet, show balance.  
- Send USDC on L2 (Base/Stellar in later milestones).  
- Request Mobile Money cash-out (stubbed today; partner-integrated in Phase 1).

**5. Why Now**  
- Mobile money is ubiquitous; telco APIs mature.  
- Stablecoin rails: predictable cost, real-time.  
- Strong early-user segments (remittances, B2C payouts, freelancers).

**6. Product Overview**  
- User app: Wallet / Send / Cash-out / KYC / Receipts.  
- Admin: KPIs, limits, risk flags, webhooks.  
- Roadmap: merchant payouts, agents, FR localization, XOF expansion.

**7. Business Model**  
- Cash-out fee + FX spread for cross-border.  
- Premium SMB features (bulk payouts/API).  
- Optional agent commissions.

**8. Unit Economics**  
- Traditional remittance: 8–9% vs sub-1% all-in on-chain where feasible.  
- Illustrate per-transaction example (US $200 to XAF).  
- Sensitivity: telco/PSP fees, FX spread, L2 gas.

**9. Compliance & Risk**  
- KYC tiers; velocity/amount limits; device/geo risk.  
- Sanctions/PEP screening; SAR triggers.  
- Data retention & user consent.

**10. Market Landscape**  
- Telcos (MTN/Orange), PSPs (CamPay/Kora), exchanges/neobanks.  
- Positioning: XAF-first, compliance-forward, enterprise payouts.

**11. Team & Traction**  
- Banking, treasury, telco-PSP relationships, local ops.  
- Pilot KPIs to track: verified users, MAU, cash-out volume, take rate.

**12. Roadmap & Ask**  
- 12-month milestones (PSP integration, pilot, merchant API, expansion).  
- Budget & use of funds; key hires; regulatory engagement plan.
